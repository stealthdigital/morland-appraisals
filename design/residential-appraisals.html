Residential Appraisals

Accurate valuations for single family homes, townhouses, condos and apartments across the North Bay area.

From purchasing and mortgage financing to capital gains declaration, legal issues or debt solutions, there are many reasons why you might want an appraisal. No matter the reason, Morland Real Estate Appraisals has the residential appraisal service you need, including single family homes, townhouses, condos, apartments, and other residential buildings.

After years of appraising real estate in the North Bay area, we understand both the local market, and how to evaluate the many different types of properties. During an appraisal, we take into account the age of the home, current condition, building materials, location, market trends, and many other factors.

In addition to standard buying and selling appraisals, we also perform valuations for estate settlements, insurance, relocation, matrimonial separations, lease arbitration, and many other situations. Our accurate and precise appraisals will help you determine the true worth of a property for your exact needs.

To discuss your residential appraisal needs and specific services, reach out to us at (705) 474-3508 for more information.
